Retinal OCT Privacy Utility

Privacy / data handling utility

Project 05

This utility supported safer research use of retinal OCT exports by identifying and removing specified patient-identifying fields before any downstream handling. It was a working implementation in a research environment, it aligned the export workflow with practical privacy needs in a regulated setting, and it reduced manual de-identification effort before research use.

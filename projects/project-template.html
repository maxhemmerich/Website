[app] AI / RESEARCH TOOLS

Distill — AI-Powered Research Reader

Distill is a structured reading interface that transforms long-form web content and pasted text into document-, section-, and paragraph-level summaries while preserving source context.

What it does

  • Accepts a URL or raw text as input.
  • Groups content into logical sections.
  • Generates summaries at multiple levels of detail.
  • Keeps the reading experience organized and scannable.

Why it matters

The product is easy to explain to recruiters because it solves a clear problem: making dense online reading more navigable without stripping out context.

Stack

Next.js 14 · TypeScript · OpenAI API · Custom interface design

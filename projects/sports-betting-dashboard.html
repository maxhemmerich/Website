Sports Betting Dashboard

A dashboard focused on surfacing profitable bets, organizing signal review, and tracking outcomes in a more disciplined decision workflow.

Problem

Betting data is noisy. A useful interface needs to prioritize candidate decisions, not just dump information onto the screen.

What it shows

  • Dashboard thinking and analytical prioritization.
  • Workflow design for tracking opportunities and results.
  • A practical example of turning raw inputs into action-oriented review.

Why it matters

This project is useful for data and analytics roles because it demonstrates filtering, ranking, monitoring, and presenting information clearly.

Stack

Analytics · Monitoring · Dashboard design
